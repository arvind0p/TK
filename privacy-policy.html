Privacy Policy

How this website handles information and what you can expect when using it.

Draft for review. This page is a starting template based on how this website works. Please have it reviewed by a qualified legal professional and update the contact details before publishing.

Information we collect

When you use the enquiry form we receive the details you enter: your name, organisation, email address, phone number, area of interest and the message you write. This website does not require an account and does not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, arrange a discovery call and, if you become a client, deliver the agreed work. We do not sell your information.

How enquiries are delivered

Depending on how the site is configured, an enquiry is either sent to our email by a form service or opens as an email draft on your device. In both cases the message is handled by the relevant email or form provider.

Third-party services

Fonts are hosted on this website itself. If privacy-friendly analytics is enabled, it records aggregate page views without advertising cookies [confirm with the tool you choose]. The meeting scheduler and payment links open services run by other providers, which have their own privacy policies.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry correspondence only as long as needed for the purpose above or as required by law. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing us at the address on the Contact page.
