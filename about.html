About us

Research-led advisory, built for practical decisions.

Technisch Kaizen Services Pvt Ltd is a business consulting and technology advisory firm based in Jammu, Jammu & Kashmir, working with clients across India.

Who we are

Continuous improvement, applied to your decisions.

“Kaizen” is the Japanese idea of steady, continuous improvement. It shapes how we work: define a clear question, gather the evidence, recommend practical steps, then help you measure what changed.

We combine market research, brand and go-to-market thinking, technology consulting, AI governance, data security and compliance support, so growth and risk are considered together.
