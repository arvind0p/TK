Refund & Cancellation Policy

How this website handles information and what you can expect when using it.

Draft for review. This page is a starting template based on how this website works. Please have it reviewed by a qualified legal professional and update the contact details before publishing.

Scope

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for consulting fees paid to Technisch Kaizen Services Pvt Ltd. The specific fee, milestones and schedule for each engagement are set out in the proposal or invoice you accept.

Fees and payment

Fees are agreed in writing before work begins. Payments can be made through the secure payment link on your proposal or invoice. Payment is processed by a third-party payment gateway, and we do not store card or bank details.

Cancellation by the client

You may cancel an engagement by written notice. Fees for work already completed and for approved expenses up to the date of cancellation remain payable. [Notice period and any cancellation charge to be confirmed by the company.]

Refunds

Where an advance payment exceeds the work completed at cancellation, the balance will be refunded. [Refund timelines, such as the number of working days, to be confirmed by the company.] Fees for deliverables that have been provided are not refundable.

Failed or duplicate payments

If an amount is debited but the payment fails, or you are charged twice, contact us with the transaction reference. Verified duplicate or failed payments are refunded to the original payment method.

Contact

For questions about an invoice, cancellation or refund, email us at the address on the Contact page.
